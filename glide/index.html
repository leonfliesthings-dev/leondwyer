Glide Planner

✈️ Get the app

Plan it, fly it, log it — one app, both platforms

Build a task on the chart, brief its NOTAMs and weather, then fly it with live glide range, MacCready speed-to-fly, frequency alerts, SOS messaging and automatic flight logging.

 iOS

Join TestFlight

🤖 Android

Download APK
Download the VNC chart for the area you fly and it lives on the phone — planning and flying both work with no signal.
🌪️ SkySight lift forecasts Thermal strength and height along your route, plus a lift-strength map overlay — with a free Open-Meteo fallback when SkySight is unavailable.
📣 NOTAMs in plain English Live NAIPS briefings for your route decoded from contractions into readable language, pinned on the map, with TAF/METAR alongside.
🏆 WeGlide upload Recorded flights export as IGC and upload to WeGlide with your pilot profile linked.
📤 Flight exports Recorded flights export as IGC, GPX or KML, and plans import and export as JSON or CUP.
