The phone app and the web planner are companions that work completely independently — plan on the desktop and import to your phone via a JSON file, or do everything on the phone. Neither needs the other in the air.
🌪️ SkySight lift forecasts Thermal strength and height along your route, plus a lift-strength map overlay — with a free Open-Meteo fallback when SkySight is unavailable.
📣 NOTAMs in plain English Live NAIPS briefings for your route decoded from contractions into readable language, pinned on the map, with TAF/METAR alongside.
🏆 WeGlide upload Recorded flights export as IGC and upload to WeGlide with your pilot profile linked.
📤 Flight plan exports IGC, GPX, KML, printable briefing packs, and JSON hand-off between the planner and the phone app.
